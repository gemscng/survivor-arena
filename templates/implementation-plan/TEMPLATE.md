# Implementation Plan Template

## Usage
Use this template when the client needs a detailed execution blueprint for a strategic initiative. Follows a strategy engagement or can be standalone. Typical duration: 3-6 week engagement.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Every workstream has a named owner on the client side
- [ ] Timeline accounts for client team availability and holidays
- [ ] Risk register includes at least one risk per workstream
- [ ] Budget includes contingency
- [ ] Governance cadence is agreed with client sponsor
- [ ] Success metrics are measurable and have baselines
- [ ] Change management section addresses people impact
- [ ] Plan has been reviewed by Practice Lead for methodology alignment

## Delivery Notes
- Walk through the plan live with the client sponsor before distributing
- Get written sign-off on scope, timeline, and budget before execution begins
- Update the plan at each steering meeting; version-control all changes

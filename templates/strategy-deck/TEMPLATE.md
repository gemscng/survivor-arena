# Strategy Deck Template

## Usage
Use this template for engagements where the primary deliverable is a strategic recommendation to the client's leadership team. Typical duration: 2-4 week engagement.

## Structure

### Slide 1: Title & Engagement Context
- Client name and logo
- Engagement title
- Date and engagement team
- Confidentiality notice

### Slide 2: Executive Summary
- 3-5 bullet synthesis of findings and recommendations
- Expected impact (quantified where possible)
- Recommended timeline for action

### Slide 3: Current State Assessment
- Where the client is today
- Key metrics and benchmarks
- Pain points identified during discovery
- Use data from client interviews, internal docs, and market research

### Slide 4: Market & Competitive Context
- Relevant market trends
- Competitive landscape summary
- Opportunities and threats
- Source all claims; prefer primary data over secondary

### Slide 5: Strategic Options Analysis
- 2-4 options evaluated against client's criteria
- Pros/cons matrix for each option
- Risk assessment per option
- Use a consistent evaluation framework (cost, speed, risk, alignment)

### Slide 6: Recommended Strategy
- Clear articulation of the recommended path
- Why this option over alternatives
- Key assumptions and dependencies
- Success metrics and how to measure them

### Slide 7: Implementation Roadmap
- Phased approach with milestones
- Quick wins (0-30 days)
- Medium-term initiatives (30-90 days)
- Strategic plays (90-180 days)
- Owner assignments where applicable

### Slide 8: Investment & ROI
- Estimated investment required
- Expected return timeline
- Sensitivity analysis for key variables
- Comparison to cost of inaction

### Slide 9: Risks & Mitigations
- Top 3-5 risks to the strategy
- Mitigation plan for each
- Escalation triggers

### Slide 10: Next Steps & Ask
- Specific decisions needed from the client
- Proposed next engagement phase (if applicable)
- Timeline for decision

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Every recommendation is tied to evidence from discovery
- [ ] Financial estimates have stated assumptions and ranges, not point estimates
- [ ] Competitive data is sourced and dated
- [ ] Implementation roadmap is realistic given client's stated capacity
- [ ] Risks section addresses the client's top concern from discovery
- [ ] No jargon without definition; deck is readable by a non-specialist exec
- [ ] Proofread by a second team member before delivery

## Delivery Notes
- Present in person or live video; never send cold
- Allow 60 min: 30 min presentation, 30 min discussion
- Send PDF follow-up within 24 hours with any agreed amendments

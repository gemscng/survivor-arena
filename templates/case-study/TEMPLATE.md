# Case Study Template

## Usage
Complete this template at the end of every engagement. Case studies are our primary proof of capability for new business development. Aim to complete within one week of engagement close.

## 1. The Challenge (1-2 paragraphs)
What problem did the client face? Why did they need external help? What was at stake?

Write from the client's perspective. Make the problem relatable to future prospects in similar situations.

**Key questions to answer:**
- What triggered the engagement?
- What had the client already tried?
- What were the constraints (time, budget, organizational)?

---

## 2. Our Approach (2-3 paragraphs)
How did we tackle the problem? What methodology did we use? What made our approach distinctive?

**Key questions to answer:**
- What was the engagement structure (phases, timeline)?
- What research or analysis did we conduct?
- How did we collaborate with the client team?
- What tools or frameworks did we apply?

---

## 3. Key Insights (3-5 bullets)
What non-obvious findings emerged from the work? These demonstrate our analytical depth.

Each insight should follow the pattern:
- **[Insight title]:** [One sentence explaining what we found and why it mattered]

---

- [ ] No confidential information is disclosed
- [ ] Case study has been reviewed by a second team member
- [ ] Formatted for both website (short version) and pitch deck (detailed version)

---

## Process: How to Capture a Case Study

### During the Engagement
1. **Track metrics from day one.** Agree with the client on baseline measurements at kickoff.
2. **Document insights as you go.** Don't rely on memory at the end.
3. **Request a testimonial quote** during the final review meeting, when satisfaction is highest.

### After the Engagement
1. **Draft within 5 business days** of engagement close while context is fresh.
2. **Get client review** — send the draft for accuracy check and publication approval.
3. **Create two versions:**
   - **Short (250 words):** For website and social media
   - **Detailed (800-1200 words):** For pitch decks and proposals
4. **File in the case study library** with tags for industry, service type, and engagement size.

### Anonymization Rules (when client declines to be named)
- Replace company name with "[Industry] [Company Type]" (e.g., "Mid-market SaaS company")
- Remove any details that could identify the client
- Keep metrics as percentages or ranges rather than absolute numbers

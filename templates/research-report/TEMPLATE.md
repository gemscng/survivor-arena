# Research Report Template

## Usage
Use this template for market research, competitive analysis, technology landscape, or data-driven recommendation engagements. Typical duration: 1-3 week engagement.

---

## Report Metadata

**Title:** [Research topic]
**Client:** [Client name]
**Date:** [Date]
**Prepared by:** [Engagement team]
**Classification:** [Client Confidential / Internal]

---

## 1. Executive Summary
2-3 paragraphs summarizing the research question, key findings, and recommended actions. A busy executive should be able to read only this section and understand what to do.

---

## 2. Research Scope & Methodology

### 2.1 Research Questions
State the 2-4 specific questions this research answers.

### 2.2 Methodology
- **Primary research:** Interviews, surveys, data collection (specify sample size, selection criteria)
- **Secondary research:** Industry reports, public filings, databases used
- **Analysis methods:** Frameworks applied (e.g., Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, TAM/SAM/SOM)

### 2.3 Limitations
State what this research does not cover and why. Note any data gaps or potential biases.

---

## 3. Market / Landscape Overview
Provide the macro context. Size the opportunity. Identify key trends shaping the space.

### 3.1 Market Size & Growth
| Segment | Current Size | Growth Rate | Projection (3yr) | Source |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|--------|
|         |             |             |                   |        |

### 3.2 Key Trends
Number each trend. For each: what it is, why it matters to the client, and the evidence.

### 3.3 Regulatory & External Factors
Note any regulatory, economic, or geopolitical factors that affect the landscape.

---

## 4. Competitive / Comparative Analysis

### 4.1 Landscape Map
Position key players on relevant axes (e.g., capability vs. market share, price vs. quality).

### 4.2 Detailed Profiles
For each key player:
- **Overview:** What they do, scale, funding/revenue
- **Strengths:** What they do well
- **Weaknesses:** Where they fall short
- **Relevance to client:** Direct threat, potential partner, or benchmark

### 4.3 Comparison Matrix
| Dimension | Player A | Player B | Player C | Client |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|--------|
|           |          |          |          |        |

---

## 5. Findings & Insights
Synthesize the research into 3-5 actionable insights. Each insight should follow this structure:

### Insight [N]: [Title]
**Finding:** What the data shows.
**Implication:** What it means for the client.
**Evidence:** Supporting data points with sources.

---

## 7. Appendices
- Raw data tables
- Interview summaries (anonymized where required)
- Detailed methodology notes
- Source bibliography

---

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Every claim in the report has a cited source
- [ ] Market size figures include methodology note (top-down vs. bottom-up)
- [ ] Competitive profiles are factual, not speculative
- [ ] Recommendations are specific and actionable, not generic advice
- [ ] Executive summary can stand alone without the full report
- [ ] Client-specific context is woven throughout (not a generic industry report)
- [ ] Limitations section is honest about what we don't know
- [ ] Proofread and formatted consistently

## Delivery Notes
- Deliver the full report as PDF
- Present findings in a 45-min session: 25 min walkthrough, 20 min Q&A
- Offer a follow-up session if the client wants to deep-dive on specific findings

# Client Communication Cadence

## Overview

Consistent, structured communication builds trust and prevents surprises. Every engagement follows this standard cadence. The Engagement Manager owns all client communications.

---

## 1. Standard Check-in Schedule

| Touchpoint | Frequency | Format | Attendees | Duration |
|------------|-----------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Kickoff** | Once (Day 1) | Video call | Full team + client stakeholders | 60 min |
| **Weekly Status** | Weekly | Video call or async report | EM + Client sponsor | 30 min |
| **Milestone Review** | Per milestone | Video call + deliverable walkthrough | Full team + client reviewers | 60 min |
| **Executive Check-in** | Bi-weekly or monthly | Video call | CEO/EM + Client exec sponsor | 30 min |
| **Closeout** | Once (final) | Video call + written summary | Full team + client stakeholders | 60 min |

## 2. Weekly Status Report Template

Send every **Monday by 10am** (covering the prior week):

```
Subject: [Client Name] — Weekly Status [Date]

## Summary
[1-2 sentence overall status]

## Health: [Green / Yellow / Red]

## Completed This Week
- [Accomplishment 1]
- [Accomplishment 2]

## Planned Next Week
- [Task 1]
- [Task 2]

## Budget Status

```

## 3. Escalation Protocol

### Severity Levels

| Level | Definition | Response Time | Who Gets Notified |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **P1 — Critical** | Engagement at risk of failure; relationship damage; missed contractual obligation | Within 4 hours | CEO + Client exec sponsor |
| **P2 — High** | Milestone at risk; budget overrun >15%; key staffing gap | Within 24 hours | CEO + Client sponsor |
| **P3 — Medium** | Minor delay; client dissatisfaction signal; scope creep starting | Within 48 hours | Engagement Manager handles directly |

### Escalation Steps

1. **Identify** — Document the issue clearly (what, impact, urgency).
2. **Assess** — Determine severity level using the table above.
3. **Notify** — Alert the appropriate people within the response time.
4. **Propose** — Always escalate with a recommended action, not just the problem.
5. **Resolve** — Track to resolution and confirm with client.
6. **Document** — Add to engagement risk/issue log.

## 4. Communication Rules

1. **No surprises.** If something is going wrong, the client hears it from us first.
2. **Written trail.** Every significant decision or direction change is confirmed in writing (email or shared doc).
3. **Client response SLAs.** We respond to client messages within 1 business day. Acknowledge receipt even if the full answer takes longer.
4. **Meeting notes.** Every client meeting produces notes with action items, owners, and deadlines — sent within 24 hours.
5. **Feedback loops.** Ask for client feedback at each milestone review, not just at closeout.

## 5. Kickoff Agenda Template

1. Introductions and roles (10 min)
2. Engagement objectives and success criteria (15 min)
3. Scope walkthrough — in-scope and out-of-scope (10 min)
4. Timeline and milestone review (10 min)
5. Communication cadence agreement (5 min)
6. Logistics — tools, access, scheduling (5 min)
7. Q&A and next steps (5 min)

## 6. Closeout Agenda Template

1. Deliverable summary and acceptance status (15 min)
2. Outcomes vs. objectives — did we hit the mark? (15 min)
3. Lessons learned — what worked, what to improve (15 min)
4. Open items and transition plan (10 min)
5. Future opportunities discussion (5 min)

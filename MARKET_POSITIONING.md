# THEA Consulting — Market Positioning

## Where We Play

### Primary Practice Area 1: AI Strategy for the Mid-Market

**Why this market:**
- Large enterprises have McKinsey/BCG. Startups DIY or can't afford consulting. The mid-market ($10M–$500M revenue) is underserved.
- These companies have real AI budgets but no internal AI expertise.
- Decision cycles are shorter than enterprise (weeks, not quarters).
- The AI strategy market is projected at $30B+ by 2027 — the mid-market slice alone is massive.

**How we win:**
- **Speed:** We deliver in days/weeks what traditional firms take months to produce.
- **Practicality:** Our recommendations are implementable, not theoretical. We can actually build what we recommend.
- **Price point:** 50-70% less than traditional strategy firms for comparable quality.
- **No bench cost:** AI-native means we scale up/down instantly. No utilization pressure driving unnecessary upsells.

### Primary Practice Area 2: AI-Powered Research & Intelligence

**Why this market:**
- Every company needs research but few can afford dedicated analysts.
- AI agents excel at research synthesis — it's a natural strength.
- Low barrier to entry: small initial engagements that expand.
- Recurring revenue potential (retainer-based intelligence services).

**How we win:**
- **Depth:** We can process and synthesize information at a scale human analysts cannot.
- **Turnaround:** Days instead of weeks for comprehensive research.
- **Structured output:** Board-ready, decision-oriented deliverables — not data dumps.

### Secondary Practice Area: Process Automation

**Why this market:**
- Every company has manual processes they know should be automated.
- High ROI makes the sale easier — we can quantify savings.
- Natural expansion from strategy engagements ("you told us what to do, now help us do it").

**How we win:**
- **End-to-end:** We audit, design, AND build — most consultants stop at recommendations.
- **AI-native automation:** We understand AI agent workflows because we ARE AI agents.
- **Measurable outcomes:** ROI tracking built into every engagement.

# THEA Consulting — Service Catalog

## Overview

THEA is an AI-native consulting firm. Our team of specialized AI agents delivers high-quality strategic and technical consulting at speed and scale that traditional firms cannot match. We operate 24/7, produce rigorous analysis, and price on value delivered — not hours billed.

**Revenue target:** $1M USD / year
**Team:** CEO, Practice Lead, Engagement Manager, Senior Consultant

---

## Service 1: AI Strategy & Readiness Assessment

**What:** Help organizations evaluate where AI can create value, assess their readiness, and build a prioritized roadmap for adoption.

**Value proposition:** Clients get a clear, actionable AI strategy in days instead of months — with honest assessment of what will and won't work for their specific context.

**Target clients:**
- Mid-market companies ($10M–$500M revenue) exploring AI adoption
- Executives with AI budget but no internal expertise
- Companies that tried AI pilots that stalled

**Deliverables:**
1. Current-state assessment (data maturity, process mapping, team capabilities)
2. Opportunity matrix — AI use cases ranked by impact vs. feasibility
3. 90-day implementation roadmap with resource requirements
4. Executive briefing deck

**Engagement length:** 2–4 weeks
**Indicative pricing:** $25,000–$75,000 per engagement

---

## Service 2: Technical Architecture & System Design

**What:** Design scalable, production-grade technical architectures for AI/ML systems, data platforms, and software infrastructure.

**Value proposition:** Senior-level architecture expertise without the $300/hr price tag. We produce detailed, implementable designs — not PowerPoint architectures.

**Target clients:**
- Engineering teams building AI-powered products
- CTOs needing architecture review before major investments
- Startups preparing for scale

**Deliverables:**
1. Architecture decision records (ADRs)
2. System design documents with data flow diagrams
3. Technology selection matrix with trade-off analysis
4. Infrastructure cost modeling
5. Implementation specifications for engineering teams

**Engagement length:** 1–3 weeks
**Indicative pricing:** $15,000–$50,000 per engagement

**Deliverables:**

4. Strategic recommendations with supporting evidence

**Engagement length:** 1–2 weeks
**Indicative pricing:** $10,000–$40,000 per engagement

## Service 4: Process Automation & Optimization

**What:** Identify, design, and implement automation for business processes using AI agents, workflows, and integrations.

**Value proposition:** We don't just recommend automation — we build it. Clients see measurable time and cost savings within weeks.

**Target clients:**
- Operations leaders drowning in manual processes
- Companies with repetitive knowledge work (reporting, data entry, document processing)
- Teams that want AI agents for internal workflows

**Deliverables:**
1. Process audit with automation opportunity scoring
2. Workflow design documents
3. Working automation prototypes
4. ROI analysis (time saved, error reduction, cost savings)
5. Handoff documentation and maintenance guide

**Engagement length:** 2–6 weeks
**Indicative pricing:** $20,000–$80,000 per engagement

## Service 5: Fractional AI Leadership

**What:** Ongoing advisory and execution support as a fractional AI team. Retainer-based for clients who need continuous access.

**Value proposition:** A full AI consulting team on retainer for less than the cost of one senior hire. Continuous strategic guidance plus hands-on execution.

**Target clients:**
- Companies that can't justify a full-time AI team yet
- Organizations mid-transformation needing steady guidance
- Repeat clients who want ongoing support

**Deliverables:**
1. Monthly strategic review and planning sessions
2. On-demand research and analysis
3. Architecture reviews and technical guidance
4. Quarterly business reviews with measurable outcomes
5. Priority access and guaranteed response times

**Engagement length:** 3–12 month retainers
**Indicative pricing:** $8,000–$25,000/month

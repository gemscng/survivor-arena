# THEA Consulting — Pricing Model

## Pricing Philosophy

**Value-based pricing is the default.** We price based on the outcome delivered, not the hours spent. Our AI-native model means we can deliver faster than traditional firms — that speed is part of our value, not a reason to charge less.

**T&M is the fallback** for exploratory or ambiguous-scope work where value is hard to quantify upfront.

---

## Value-Based Pricing Guidelines

### How to Price an Engagement

1. **Estimate client value at stake.** What decision does this inform? What cost does it avoid? What revenue does it enable?
2. **Apply the 10:1 rule.** Our fee should be ~10% of the value we create or protect. A $500K decision warrants a $50K engagement.
3. **Anchor to outcomes, not effort.** Present pricing as "you get X deliverables that enable Y outcome" — not "we'll spend Z hours."
4. **Offer tiered options.** Give clients Good / Better / Best so they self-select scope and budget.

## T&M Fallback Rate Card

Use T&M only when:
- Scope is genuinely unclear and discovery is needed before fixed pricing
- Client insists on hourly (some procurement processes require it)
- Short-term, ad-hoc advisory requests

| Role | Hourly Rate | Daily Rate |
|------|------------|------------|
| Practice Lead | $350/hr | $2,500/day |
| Senior Consultant | $275/hr | $2,000/day |

**Minimum engagement:** $5,000 or 2 days, whichever is greater.

## Discounting Policy

- **No discounting below 15% of listed price** without CEO approval.
- **Volume discount:** 10% off for 3+ engagements committed upfront.
- **Retainer discount:** Monthly retainer pricing already reflects a 15-20% discount vs. project-based pricing.
- **Pilot pricing:** Offer a reduced-scope "pilot" at a lower fixed price to prove value. Never discount the full engagement as a "trial."

---

## Payment Terms

- **Project work:** 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. For engagements >$50K: 40/30/30 (upfront/midpoint/delivery).
- **Retainers:** Monthly in advance. Net 15.
- **T&M:** Invoiced bi-weekly. Net 30.
- **Late payment:** 1.5% monthly interest after 30 days overdue.

## Proposal Template Structure

Every proposal should include:
1. **Understanding** — Restate the client's problem in our words (proves we listened)
2. **Approach** — How we'll solve it (reference DADR methodology)
3. **Deliverables** — Specific, tangible outputs
4. **Timeline** — Phase-by-phase with milestones
5. **Investment** — Tiered pricing options (Good/Better/Best)
6. **About THEA** — Brief firm overview and relevant experience
7. **Terms** — Payment schedule, revision policy, IP ownership

# THEA Consulting — Quality Standards

## Quality Philosophy

Every deliverable must be **defensible and actionable**. Defensible means the analysis is rigorous, the data is sourced, and the logic is sound. Actionable means the client can act on it without needing to hire another consultant to interpret our work.

---

## Deliverable Quality Checklist

Every client-facing deliverable must pass ALL of these checks before delivery:

### 1. Accuracy & Rigor
- [ ] All claims are supported by evidence or clearly labeled as assumptions
- [ ] Data sources are cited and verifiable
- [ ] Numbers have been cross-checked (no calculation errors)
- [ ] Competitive/market data is current (within 6 months unless historical)
- [ ] Technical recommendations are feasible with the client's stated constraints

### 2. Actionability
- [ ] Recommendations are specific (not "consider improving X" — instead "implement X by doing Y")
- [ ] Each recommendation includes: what to do, who should own it, expected timeline, expected outcome
- [ ] Prioritization is clear (what to do first, second, third)
- [ ] Dependencies between recommendations are explicitly stated
- [ ] Client can start executing without additional consulting

### 3. Clarity & Structure
- [ ] Executive summary on page 1 — a busy CEO can get the point in 60 seconds
- [ ] Logical flow: situation > analysis > findings > recommendations
- [ ] No jargon without definition; no acronyms without expansion on first use
- [ ] Visual aids (diagrams, tables, charts) where they clarify — not decoration
- [ ] Consistent formatting throughout

### 4. Client-Specific
- [ ] Uses client's terminology and references their specific context
- [ ] Addresses the specific questions raised in the engagement charter
- [ ] Acknowledges constraints and works within them (not idealized recommendations)
- [ ] Names specific people, teams, or systems where relevant

### 5. Professionalism
- [ ] No grammatical or spelling errors
- [ ] Consistent branding and formatting
- [ ] All sections complete (no TODOs, placeholders, or "TBD" in final deliverables)
- [ ] File naming follows convention: `THEA_[ClientName]_[Deliverable]_[Date]`
- [ ] Confidentiality notice included where appropriate

## Internal Review Process

**Every deliverable goes through peer review before client delivery.**

### Review Roles

| Reviewer | Focus |
|----------|-------|
| **Practice Lead** | Methodology adherence, analytical rigor, strategic soundness |
| **Engagement Manager** | Client alignment, scope compliance, communication clarity |
| **Senior Consultant** | Technical accuracy, feasibility, implementation detail |

### Review Protocol

1. Author completes deliverable and self-reviews against the quality checklist
2. Author submits for peer review with context notes ("I'm uncertain about X", "client is sensitive about Y")
3. Reviewer reviews within 24 hours using the quality checklist
4. Reviewer provides written feedback: must-fix (blocking) vs. should-fix (recommended) vs. nice-to-have
5. Author addresses all must-fix items, considers should-fix items
6. Second reviewer confirms must-fix items are resolved
7. Deliverable cleared for client delivery

### Review Standards

- **Must-fix:** Factual errors, missing scope items, unclear recommendations, broken formatting
- **Should-fix:** Tone issues, suboptimal structure, missing context, weak evidence
- **Nice-to-have:** Style preferences, additional examples, enhanced visuals

## What Makes Our Work Defensible

1. **Methodology transparency:** We show our work. Clients see how we arrived at conclusions.
2. **Source documentation:** Every major claim traces back to a source. We maintain source logs.
3. **Assumption registers:** We explicitly list what we assumed and why. No hidden premises.
4. **Scenario analysis:** Where uncertainty exists, we present multiple scenarios — not false precision.
5. **Limitation acknowledgment:** We state what our analysis does NOT cover. No overreach.

## Engagement Health Metrics

Track these per engagement to ensure quality:

| Metric | Target | Red Flag |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Client NPS | >8 | <6 |
| Deliverable revision rounds | <=1 | >2 |
| Scope changes | 0 | >1 unplanned |
| Timeline adherence | On-time or early | >3 days late |
| Follow-on interest | Client asks about next engagement | Client goes silent |

## Continuous Improvement

- **After every engagement:** Run retro, update case study library, flag methodology improvements
- **Monthly:** Review aggregate quality metrics across all engagements
- **Quarterly:** Update methodology, templates, and quality standards based on learnings
- **Annually:** Full practice review — are our services still aligned with market demand?

# THEA Consulting — Engagement Methodology

## The THEA Method

Every engagement follows four phases: **Discover, Analyze, Deliver, Review (DADR)**.

This framework ensures consistency, quality, and client confidence regardless of service type.

---

## Phase 1: Discover (Days 1–3)

**Objective:** Understand the client's real problem, context, and success criteria.

**Activities:**
- Intake call / kickoff session with client stakeholders
- Document review (existing materials, data, prior work)
- Stakeholder mapping — who decides, who influences, who blocks
- Constraint identification (timeline, budget, technical, political)
- Success criteria alignment — what does "done well" look like?

**Templates:**
- `templates/discovery-brief.md` — Structured intake questionnaire
- `templates/stakeholder-map.md` — Decision-maker and influencer grid
- `templates/engagement-charter.md` — Scope, timeline, success criteria, communication plan

**Exit criteria:** Signed engagement charter with mutual agreement on scope, deliverables, timeline, and success metrics.

**Owner:** Engagement Manager (primary), Practice Lead (methodology guidance)

---

## Phase 2: Analyze (Days 3–10)

**Objective:** Do the deep work. Research, model, design, or build depending on engagement type.

**Activities:**
- Primary research and data gathering
- Analysis and synthesis (frameworks, models, comparisons)
- Draft deliverable creation
- Internal quality review (peer review before client sees anything)
- Interim check-in with client (progress update, early findings, course correction)

**Templates:**
- `templates/analysis-framework.md` — Structured approach for each service type
- `templates/interim-update.md` — Client progress communication template
- `templates/internal-review-checklist.md` — Peer review standards

**Exit criteria:** Draft deliverables complete, internally reviewed, interim client alignment confirmed.

**Owner:** Senior Consultant (execution), Practice Lead (quality review)

---

## Phase 3: Deliver (Days 10–14)

**Objective:** Present findings and recommendations. Ensure client can act on them.

**Activities:**
- Final deliverable polish and formatting
- Client presentation / walkthrough session
- Q&A and challenge session (we welcome pushback)
- Revision cycle (one round of revisions included in standard pricing)
- Handoff of all materials, data, and supporting documentation

**Templates:**
- `templates/deliverable-package.md` — Standard deliverable structure
- `templates/presentation-outline.md` — Client presentation framework
- `templates/handoff-checklist.md` — Complete transfer checklist

**Exit criteria:** Client accepts deliverables, all materials transferred, feedback captured.

**Owner:** Practice Lead (presentation), Engagement Manager (logistics), Senior Consultant (materials)

---

## Phase 4: Review (Days 14–21)

**Objective:** Assess engagement quality, capture learnings, and identify follow-on opportunities.

**Activities:**
- Client satisfaction check (structured feedback)
- Internal retrospective — what worked, what didn't, what to improve
- Case study draft (anonymized, for internal use and marketing)
- Follow-on opportunity identification
- Methodology update if lessons warrant it

**Templates:**
- `templates/client-feedback.md` — Post-engagement satisfaction survey
- `templates/retro.md` — Internal retrospective format
- `templates/case-study.md` — Anonymized engagement summary

**Exit criteria:** Retro completed, case study drafted, CRM updated with follow-on opportunities.

**Owner:** Engagement Manager (feedback + CRM), Practice Lead (retro + methodology updates)

---

## Role Responsibilities per Phase

| Phase | CEO | Practice Lead | Engagement Manager | Senior Consultant |
|-------|-----|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Discover | Sponsor intro | Methodology guidance | **Lead**: intake, charter | Support |
| Analyze | — | **Quality review** | Client comms | **Lead**: execution |
| Deliver | Executive sponsor | **Lead**: presentation | Logistics | Materials prep |
| Review | Pipeline review | Retro + methodology | **Lead**: feedback + CRM | Case study |

## Engagement Types by Service

| Service | Discover | Analyze | Deliver | Review |
|---------|----------|---------|---------|--------|
| AI Strategy | Stakeholder interviews, data audit | Opportunity mapping, readiness scoring | Strategy deck + roadmap | Implementation check-in |
| Technical Architecture | Requirements gathering, system inventory | Design exploration, trade-off analysis | ADRs + design docs | Architecture review session |
| Research & Intelligence | Question framing, source identification | Deep research, synthesis | Research brief + recommendations | Accuracy validation |
| Process Automation | Process observation, pain point mapping | Workflow design, prototype building | Working automation + docs | Performance measurement |
| Fractional Leadership | Monthly priority setting | Ongoing analysis and advisory | Continuous deliverables | Quarterly business review |

---

## Communication Standards

- **Response time:** Within 4 hours during business days
- **Status updates:** At minimum, at phase transitions
- **Escalation:** Any scope risk flagged to Engagement Manager within 24 hours
- **Documentation:** All client-facing work in shared workspace; nothing lives only in email

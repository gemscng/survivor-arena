# Staffing Model

## Overview

Match the right people to the right engagements. Track utilization to ensure profitability and prevent burnout.

---

## 1. Engagement Roles

| Role | Responsibilities | Typical Allocation | Target Bill Rate |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Engagement Manager** | Client relationship, delivery oversight, scope management, escalation | 15-25% per engagement | $[Rate]/hr |
| **Lead Consultant** | Technical/strategic leadership, deliverable quality, methodology execution | 50-80% per engagement | $[Rate]/hr |
| **Consultant** | Analysis, execution, deliverable creation, research | 80-100% per engagement | $[Rate]/hr |
| **Subject Matter Expert** | Specialized input on specific topics, review | 5-15% per engagement | $[Rate]/hr |

## 2. Staffing Decision Framework

When assigning team members to engagements, evaluate:

| Factor | Weight | Question |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Domain fit** | High | Does the consultant have relevant expertise for this client's industry/problem? |
| **Availability** | High | Can they commit the required allocation within the engagement timeline? |
| **Client relationship** | Medium | Have they worked with this client before? Is continuity valuable? |
| **Development** | Low | Does this engagement offer growth opportunities for the consultant? |
| **Utilization balance** | Medium | Are we spreading work evenly or overloading someone? |

## 3. Utilization Tracking

### Target Utilization

| Role | Target Billable Utilization | Rationale |
|------|---------------------------|-----------|
| Engagement Manager | 50-60% | Remaining time: BD, proposals, internal ops |
| Lead Consultant | 65-75% | Remaining time: methodology development, mentoring |
| Consultant | 75-85% | Remaining time: training, internal projects |

### Utilization Calculation

```
Billable Utilization = Billable Hours / Available Hours x 100

Available Hours = Working Days x 8 hrs - PTO - Company Holidays
```

### Tracking Cadence

- **Weekly:** Each team member logs hours by engagement
- **Monthly:** Engagement Manager reviews utilization report, flags anyone below 50% or above 90%
- **Quarterly:** Review utilization trends, adjust staffing or sales pipeline accordingly

### Alert Thresholds

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Utilization < 50% for 2+ weeks | Flag to CEO — need more pipeline or internal project assignment |
| Utilization > 90% for 2+ weeks | Risk of burnout — redistribute or bring in support |
| Consultant unassigned for > 1 week | Immediate escalation — revenue at risk |

## 4. Capacity Planning

### Engagement Capacity Model

```
Total team capacity (hrs/week) = Sum of each person's available hours x target utilization

Current capacity: [Calculate based on team size]
Committed capacity: [Sum of current engagement allocations]
Available capacity: Total - Committed
```

### Pipeline-to-Capacity Check

Before signing a new SOW:
1. Map required roles and hours against available capacity
2. If capacity gap exists: identify who can shift, or flag hire/contractor need
3. Never commit >90% of any individual's capacity across engagements

## 5. Bench Management

When a consultant is between engagements:

1. **Internal projects** — Methodology development, template improvement, case study writing
2. **Business development support** — Proposal writing, prospect research, demo prep
3. **Training** — Skill development aligned with practice areas
4. **Prospecting** — If bench extends beyond 2 weeks, the person actively supports pipeline development

## 6. Staffing Change Protocol

| Change | Notice Required | Approval |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| Swap consultant (same level) | 5 business days | Engagement Manager + client notification |
| Replace lead consultant | 10 business days | CEO + client approval required |
| Add team member | 3 business days | Engagement Manager (within SOW budget) |
| Remove team member | 5 business days | Engagement Manager + client notification |
| Subcontractor addition | Per SOW terms | Client approval typically required |
